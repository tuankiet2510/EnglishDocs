## PART A: PHONETICS & STRESS (Ngữ Âm & Trọng Âm)

<QuizItem
  :number="1"
  question="Choose the word whose underlined part is pronounced differently from that of the others."
  options="A. visited | B. decided | C. wanted | D. looked"
  correct="D"
  explanation="'looked' phát âm đuôi -ed là /t/ vì tận cùng là âm vô thanh /k/. Các từ còn lại tận cùng là /t/ và /d/ nên phát âm là /ɪd/ ('visited', 'decided', 'wanted')."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="2"
  question="Choose the word whose underlined part is pronounced differently from that of the others."
  options="A. desks | B. maps | C. pens | D. books"
  correct="C"
  explanation="'pens' phát âm đuôi -s là /z/ vì tận cùng là nguyên âm/phụ âm hữu thanh /n/. Các từ còn lại tận cùng là âm vô thanh /k/, /p/ nên phát âm là /s/."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="3"
  question="Choose the word that differs from the other three in the position of primary stress."
  options="A. culture | B. prevent | C. custom | D. beauty"
  correct="B"
  explanation="'pre'vent' là động từ 2 âm tiết có trọng âm rơi vào âm tiết thứ 2. Các danh từ 2 âm tiết còn lại đều có trọng âm rơi vào âm tiết thứ 1 ('culture, 'custom, 'beauty)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="4"
  question="Choose the word that differs from the other three in the position of primary stress."
  options="A. traditional | B. environment | C. pollution | D. celebration"
  correct="D"
  explanation="'cele'bration' có trọng âm rơi vào âm tiết thứ 3 (trước hậu tố -tion). Các từ còn lại đều có trọng âm rơi vào âm tiết thứ 2: tra'ditional, en'vironment, pol'lution."
/>

## PART B: VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR (Từ Vựng & Ngữ Pháp)

<QuizItem

## PART D: WRITING & SENTENCE TRANSFORMATION (Viết & Biến Đổi Câu)

<QuizItem
  :number="33"
  question="&quot;Why don't we plant more trees around our school yard?&quot; said Phong.
Choose the sentence that has the closest meaning:"
  options="A. Phong suggested planting more trees around their school yard. | B. Phong refused to plant trees around the yard. | C. Phong asked why they had planted trees. | D. Phong denied planting more trees around the yard."
  correct="A"
  explanation="Cấu trúc đề xuất 'Why don't we + V...?' được viết lại bằng: S + suggest + V-ing -> Phong suggested planting more trees around their school yard."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="34"
  question="The exam was so difficult that none of the students could get a maximum score.
Choose the sentence that has the closest meaning:"
  options="A. It was such a difficult exam that none of the students could get a maximum score. | B. The exam was easy enough for all students to get high marks. | C. Although the exam was difficult, everyone got top marks. | D. It was too difficult an exam for none of the students to do."
  correct="A"
  explanation="Biến đổi 'so + adj + that' sang 'such + a/an + adj + noun + that': 'The exam was so difficult that...' -> 'It was such a difficult exam that...'"
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="35"
  question="He didn't have a map, so he got lost in the city centre.
Choose the sentence that has the closest meaning:"
  options="A. If he had had a map, he wouldn't have got lost in the city centre. | B. If he has a map, he won't get lost in the city centre. | C. Unless he had a map, he would get lost. | D. If he didn't have a map, he would get lost."
  correct="A"
  explanation="Tình huống trong quá khứ ('didn't have', 'got lost') được viết lại bằng Câu điều kiện loại 3 (trái với thực tế quá khứ): If + S + had + V3/ed, S + would(n't) + have + V3/ed."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="36"
  question="They last organized an international firework festival three years ago.
Choose the sentence that has the closest meaning:"
  options="A. They haven't organized an international firework festival for three years. | B. They have organized an international firework festival for three years. | C. They didn't organize an international firework festival since three years. | D. It has been three years since they last organize an international festival."
  correct="A"
  explanation="'S + last + V2/ed + time + ago' = 'S + haven't/hasn't + V3/ed + for + time'. Chọn A."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="37"
  question="I don't know the answer to this challenging math puzzle.
Choose the sentence that has the closest meaning:"
  options="A. I wish I knew the answer to this challenging math puzzle. | B. I wish I know the answer to this puzzle. | C. I wish I had known the answer yesterday. | D. If only I will know the answer."
  correct="A"
  explanation="Ước điều trái với hiện tại: S + wish + S + V(quá khứ đơn). 'don't know' chuyển thành 'knew'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="38"
  question="Although the traffic was heavy, they arrived at the airport on time.
Choose the sentence that has the closest meaning:"
  options="A. In spite of the heavy traffic, they arrived at the airport on time. | B. Despite the traffic was heavy, they arrived on time. | C. Because of the heavy traffic, they arrived on time. | D. Although heavy traffic, they arrived on time."
  correct="A"
  explanation="Chuyển từ 'Although + clause' sang 'In spite of / Despite + noun phrase': In spite of the heavy traffic, they arrived at the airport on time."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="39"
  question="People believe that regular exercise improves mental health and memory.
Choose the best passive voice sentence:"
  options="A. It is believed that regular exercise improves mental health and memory. | B. Regular exercise was believed to improve mental health. | C. It was believed that regular exercise improves mental health. | D. People are believed to improve mental health by regular exercise."
  correct="A"
  explanation="Câu bị động khách quan (Impersonal passive): 'People believe that S + V' -> 'It is believed that S + V'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="40"
  question="The Dragon Bridge breathes fire and water on weekend evenings. It attracts thousands of tourists.
