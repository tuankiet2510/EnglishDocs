## PART A: PHONETICS (1.0 pt)

### Section I. Choose the word that has the underlined letter(s) pronounced differently from that of the others. (0.5 pt)

<QuizItem
  :number="1"
  question="Chọn từ có phần gạch chân phát âm khác với các từ còn lại:"
  options="A. book**s** | B. root**s** | C. ball**s** | D. hope**s**"
  correct="C"
  explanation="balls phát âm là /z/. Ba từ còn lại books, roots, hopes kết thúc bằng các âm vô thanh /k, t, p/ nên phát âm là /s/."
/>

  options="A. **g**ossip | B. **g**ently | C. **g**arage | D. **g**raduated"
  correct="B"
  explanation="gently phát âm là /dʒ/. Ba từ còn lại gossip, garage, graduated phát âm là /ɡ/."
/>

## PART B: STRUCTURES AND VOCABULARY (3.0 pts)

### Section I. Choose the letter A, B, C, or D to indicate the correct answer to each of the following questions. (2.0 pts)

<QuizItem
  :number="5"
  question="I have had the roof of my house _____________."
  options="A. to replace | B. replace | C. replaced | D. been replaced"
  correct="C"
  explanation="Cấu trúc nhờ vả / truyền khiến bị động: have + something + V3/ed (have the roof replaced)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="6"
  question="Due to severe weather conditions, they had to wait long hours _____________ the airport."
  options="A. in | B. at | C. from | D. on"
  correct="B"
  explanation="Dùng giới từ 'at' chỉ vị trí tại một địa điểm công cộng cụ thể (at the airport)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="7"
  question="What beautiful eyes _____________!"
  options="A. does she have | B. she has | C. has she | D. she doesn’t have"
  correct="B"
  explanation="Cấu trúc câu cảm thán với What: What + (a/an) + adj + noun + S + V! (không đảo trợ động từ)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="8"
  question="My mother is looking up the number in the telephone _____________."
  options="A. directors | B. directly | C. direction | D. directory"
  correct="D"
  explanation="Cụm danh từ: telephone directory (danh bạ điện thoại)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="9"
  question="I can’t find my wallet, Tom. - Don’t worry. I’ll help you to _____________ it."
  options="A. look for | B. take care of | C. put on | D. turn off"
  correct="A"
  explanation="look for = search for (tìm kiếm ví tiền)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="10"
  question="I haven’t seen Tom _____________ he got married."
  options="A. before | B. until | C. since | D. although"
  correct="C"
  explanation="Thì Hiện tại hoàn thành (haven't seen) + since + mệnh đề Quá khứ đơn (got married)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="11"
  question="If Jim _____________ the music so loud, the neighbours wouldn’t have called the police."
  options="A. has played | B. didn’t play | C. was playing | D. hadn’t played"
  correct="D"
  explanation="Câu điều kiện loại 3 diễn tả sự việc trái với thực tế trong quá khứ: If + S + had(n't) + V3/ed, S + would(n't) have + V3/ed."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="12"
  question="The children don’t like living in the countryside, _____________?"
  options="A. do the children | B. don’t they | C. are they | D. do they"
  correct="D"
  explanation="Câu hỏi đuôi: Mệnh đề trước phủ định (don't like) -> phần đuôi khẳng định dùng đại từ nhân xưng thay thế 'they' (do they)."
/>

## PART C: READING AND COMPREHENSION (3.0 pts)

### Section I. Cloze Reading Passage (1.0 pt)

<div class="reading-passage-box">
  <div class="passage-badge">📖 ĐOẠN VĂN ĐIỀN TỪ: LỊCH SỬ CHIẾC TIVI</div>
  <p>Television is one of man's most important means of communication. It brings pictures and sounds from around the world into millions of homes. A person with a television set can sit in his house and watch the president make a speech or visit a foreign country. He can see a war being fought and watch government leaders try to bring about peace. Through television, home viewers can see and learn about people, places, and things in faraway lands. TV even takes its viewers out of this world. It brings them coverage of America's astronauts as the astronauts explore outer space.</p>
</div>

## PART D: WRITING (3.0 pts)

### Section I. Sentence Transformation (2.0 pts)

<QuizItem
  :number="26"
  type="transform"
  question="“Why don’t we go for a walk along My Khe beach?” Peter said."
  targetPrompt="Peter suggested..."
  sampleAnswer="Peter suggested going for a walk along My Khe beach."
  explanation="Cấu trúc đề xuất: Why don't we + V? -> S + suggested + V-ing."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="27"
  type="transform"
  question="They have just built a new suspension bridge across the Tra Khuc River."
  targetPrompt="A new suspension bridge..."
  sampleAnswer="A new suspension bridge has just been built across the Tra Khuc River."
  explanation="Chuyển sang câu bị động thì Hiện tại hoàn thành: S + have/has + just + been + V3/ed."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="28"
  type="transform"
  question="He didn't have enough money, so he couldn't buy that dictionary."
  targetPrompt="If he had..."
  sampleAnswer="If he had had enough money, he could have bought that dictionary."
  explanation="Câu điều kiện loại 3 (trái thực tế quá khứ): If + had + had, S + could have + V3."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="29"
  type="transform"
  question="Although it rained heavily, the students arrived at school on time."
  targetPrompt="Despite..."
  sampleAnswer="Despite the heavy rain, the students arrived at school on time."
  explanation="Đổi Although + clause -> Despite + noun phrase (the heavy rain)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="30"
  type="transform"
  question="I haven't talked to my former teacher for three months."
  targetPrompt="The last time..."
  sampleAnswer="The last time I talked to my former teacher was three months ago."
  explanation="Quy đổi S + haven't + V3 + for + time -> The last time S + V2 + was + time + ago."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="31"
  type="transform"
  question="“Can you help me with this heavy luggage, please?” Mary asked Tom."
  targetPrompt="Mary asked Tom to..."
  sampleAnswer="Mary asked Tom to help her with that heavy luggage."
  explanation="Tường thuật câu yêu cầu: S + asked + O + to V; đổi me -> her, this -> that."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="32"
  type="transform"
  question="The man is an experienced electrical engineer. You spoke to him yesterday."
  targetPrompt="The man whom..."
  sampleAnswer="The man whom you spoke to yesterday is an experienced electrical engineer."
  explanation="Nối câu bằng đại từ quan hệ tân ngữ chỉ người 'whom'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="33"
  type="transform"
  question="She is too young to ride a motorcycle on the street."
  targetPrompt="She is not old..."
  sampleAnswer="She is not old enough to ride a motorcycle on the street."
  explanation="Quy đổi too + adj + to V -> not + adj (trái nghĩa) + enough + to V."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="34"
  type="transform"
  question="People say that this historic temple was built in the 17th century."
  targetPrompt="This historic temple is said..."
  sampleAnswer="This historic temple is said to have been built in the 17th century."
  explanation="Bị động kép: People say (HTĐ) that ... was built (QKĐ) -> is said to have been + V3."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="35"
  type="transform"
  question="I don’t have a computer at home, so I cannot attend online classes."
  targetPrompt="I wish..."
  sampleAnswer="I wish I had a computer at home so that I could attend online classes."
  explanation="Câu ước ở hiện tại: S + wish + S + V2/ed."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="36"
  type="transform"
  question="Because he was careless, he lost his passport at the railway station."
  targetPrompt="Because of..."
  sampleAnswer="Because of his carelessness, he lost his passport at the railway station."
  explanation="Quy đổi Because + S + be + adj -> Because of + possessive + noun (his carelessness)."
/>
