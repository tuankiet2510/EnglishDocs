## PART D: SENTENCE TRANSFORMATION & WRITING (3.0 pts)

### Section I. Rewrite the sentences so that the meaning stays the same. (2.0 pts)

<QuizItem
  :number="25"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="This electronic device is very user-friendly; even seniors can master it quickly."
  promptWord="This electronic device is user-friendly enough"
  correct="for seniors to master quickly."
  explanation="Cấu trúc: S + be + adj + enough + for O + to V-inf (user-friendly enough for seniors to master quickly)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="26"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="My brother was researching natural wonders of the world when I entered his room."
  promptWord="While my brother"
  correct="was researching natural wonders of the world, I entered his room."
  explanation="Biến đổi với liên từ While: While + thì quá khứ tiếp diễn (was researching), mệnh đề sau chia quá khứ đơn (entered)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="27"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="“You should choose a job that matches your personal interests,” Sarah told me."
  promptWord="Sarah advised that"
  correct="I should choose a job that matches my personal interests."
  explanation="Cấu trúc câu khuyên bảo gián tiếp: S + advise + that + S + should + V-inf."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="28"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="Local communities in this remote area don't have access to high-speed internet."
  promptWord="We wish"
  correct="local communities in this remote area had access to high-speed internet."
  explanation="Cấu trúc câu ước không có thật ở hiện tại: S + wish + S + V-ed/quá khứ đơn (had access to...)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="29"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="Duong prepared very carefully for her speaking test, but she still felt quite nervous."
  promptWord="Although"

<QuizItem
  :number="30"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="Nguyen turned down the offer to become project leader because he was busy with IELTS."
