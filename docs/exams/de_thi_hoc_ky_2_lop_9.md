## PART A: PHONETICS & STRESS (Ngữ Âm & Trọng Âm)

<QuizItem
  :number="1"
  question="Choose the word whose underlined part is pronounced differently from that of the others."
  options="A. explored | B. travelled | C. launched | D. orbit"
  correct="C"
  explanation="'launched' có tận cùng là âm vô thanh /tʃ/ nên phát âm đuôi -ed là /t/ (/lɔːntʃt/). Các từ còn lại phát âm là /d/ ('explored' /ɪkˈsplɔːd/, 'travelled' /ˈtrævld/)."
/>

## PART D: WRITING & SENTENCE TRANSFORMATION (Viết & Biến Đổi Câu)

<QuizItem
  :number="33"
  question="People believe that solar energy will become the primary power source for households.
→ It is believed that ____________________________________________________"
  correct="It is believed that solar energy will become the primary power source for households."
  explanation="Bị động khách quan: People believe that S + V -> It is believed that S + V."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="34"
  question="I can't speak English fluently, so I cannot apply for an international scholarship.
→ If I could ____________________________________________________"
  correct="If I could speak English fluently, I could apply for an international scholarship."
  explanation="Câu điều kiện loại 2 (giả định trái với hiện tại): If I could speak..., I could apply..."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="35"
  question="&quot;Have you ever visited Son Doong cave?&quot; David asked Mai.
→ David asked Mai ____________________________________________________"
  correct="David asked Mai if / whether she had ever visited Son Doong cave."
  explanation="Câu gián tiếp câu hỏi Yes/No: S + asked + O + if/whether + S + had + V3/ed."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="36"
  question="The environmental club meets every Saturday. Its leader is passionate about wildlife conservation.
→ The environmental club, whose ____________________________________________________"
  correct="The environmental club, whose leader is passionate about wildlife conservation, meets every Saturday."
  explanation="Mệnh đề quan hệ sở hữu dùng 'whose leader' (trưởng câu lạc bộ)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="37"
  question="They last upgraded their digital computer lab two years ago.
→ They haven't ____________________________________________________"
  correct="They haven't upgraded their digital computer lab for two years."
  explanation="Chuyển từ thì Quá khứ đơn sang Hiện tại hoàn thành phủ định: haven't upgraded... for two years."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="38"
  question="Unless you submit your assignment on time, the teacher will deduct points.
→ If you do ____________________________________________________"
  correct="If you do not submit your assignment on time, the teacher will deduct points."
  explanation="Quy đổi Unless = If... not: If you do not submit your assignment on time..."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="39"
  question="The mountain was so steep that only experienced hikers could reach the summit.
→ It was such ____________________________________________________"
  correct="It was such a steep mountain that only experienced hikers could reach the summit."
  explanation="Biến đổi 'so + adj + that' thành 'such + a/an + adj + noun + that'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="40"
  question="They look forward to travelling to Ha Long Bay next summer vacation.
→ They are excited ____________________________________________________"
  correct="They are excited about travelling to Ha Long Bay next summer vacation."
  explanation="Cụm từ đồng nghĩa: 'look forward to + V-ing' = 'be excited about + V-ing' (háo hức đi đâu)."
/>
