## PART A: PHONETICS & STRESS (Ngữ Âm & Trọng Âm)

<QuizItem
  :number="1"
  question="Choose the word having the underlined part pronounced differently in each line."
  options="A. community | B. bustling | C. hunt | D. suck"
  correct="A"
  explanation="'community' chữ 'u' phát âm là /juː/. Các từ còn lại phát âm là /ʌ/ ('bustling', 'hunt', 'suck')."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="2"
  question="Choose the word having the underlined part pronounced differently in each line."
  options="A. ghost | B. office | C. long | D. modern"
  correct="A"
  explanation="'ghost' chữ 'o' phát âm là /əʊ/ (/ɡəʊst/). Các từ còn lại phát âm là /ɒ/ ('office', 'long', 'modern')."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="3"
  question="Choose the word having the underlined part pronounced differently in each line."
  options="A. chemistry | B. teach | C. speech | D. exchange"
  correct="A"
  explanation="'chemistry' nhóm 'ch' phát âm là /k/ (/ˈkemɪstri/). Các từ còn lại phát âm là /tʃ/ ('teach', 'speech', 'exchange')."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="4"
  question="Choose a word in each line that has a different stress pattern."

/>

<QuizItem

## PART B: VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR (Từ Vựng & Ngữ Pháp)

<QuizItem
  :number="6"
  question="When the police came, the thief ______ on the roof of the house."
  options="A. is running | B. was running | C. ran | D. runs"
  correct="B"
  explanation="Hành động đang diễn ra trong quá khứ khi hành động khác ập đến ('When the police came'): was running."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="7"
  question="Nam: “What about using the Internet to play this game?” – Tuan: “______”"
  options="A. Yes, good idea! | B. Thanks | C. I don’t think so | D. I am busy playing it"
  correct="A"
  explanation="Đồng ý với lời đề xuất 'What about...?': 'Yes, good idea!' (Ý hay đấy!)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="8"
  question="This test is ______ than what we thought."
  options="A. more easily | B. the easier | C. the easiest | D. easier"
  correct="D"
  explanation="So sánh hơn của tính từ ngắn 'easy' sau 'is': easier than..."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="9"
  question="Mr Buong saw Elizabeth II on TV, ______ he admired so much."
  options="A. which | B. who | C. that | D. whom"
  correct="D"
  explanation="Mệnh đề quan hệ không xác định (sau dấu phẩy) chỉ người làm tân ngữ cho động từ 'admired': dùng 'whom' (hoặc 'who')."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="10"
  question="If Alex ______ up the word in the dictionary, he ______ the meaning of it."
  options="A. looks / will know | B. looked / will know | C. looks / would know | D. looks / won’t know"
  correct="A"
  explanation="Câu điều kiện loại 1: If + S + V(hiện tại đơn), S + will + V(nguyên thể) -> 'looks / will know'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="11"
  question="Bun Cha, Pho, Hu Tieu are examples of famous Vietnamese ______."
  options="A. speciality | B. handicraft | C. pottery | D. costume"
  correct="A"
  explanation="'speciality' = món ăn đặc sản."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="12"
  question="The road is narrow, so ______ traffic congestion often occurs at rush hour."
  options="A. much | B. many | C. any | D. few"
  correct="A"
  explanation="'traffic congestion' là danh từ không đếm được -> Dùng 'much'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="13"
  question="The villagers have to ______ with numerous environmental challenges."
  options="A. cope | B. keep | C. catch | D. live"
  correct="A"
  explanation="Cụm động từ: 'cope with' = đối phó, đương đầu với khó khăn."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="14"
  question="She asked me whether I ______ to the craft exhibition the previous day."
  options="A. had gone | B. went | C. have gone | D. go"
  correct="A"
  explanation="Trong câu gián tiếp có 'the previous day' (chuyển từ yesterday): lùi thì thành Quá khứ hoàn thành 'had gone'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="15"
  question="The children wish there ______ more green parks in their neighborhood."
  options="A. were | B. are | C. will be | D. have been"
  correct="A"
  explanation="Câu ước ở hiện tại: dùng 'were' cho tất cả các ngôi."
/>

## PART C: READING COMPREHENSION (Đọc Hiểu Văn Bản)

<QuizItem
  :number="16"
  question="Traditional craft villages play an important (16) ______ in preserving Vietnamese cultural heritage."
  options="A. role | B. part | C. game | D. job"
  correct="A"
  explanation="Cụm từ cố định: 'play an important role in doing sth' (đóng vai trò quan trọng trong việc gì)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="17"
  question="Skilled artisans use local natural materials (17) ______ bamboo, silk, and clay."
  options="A. such as | B. for example | C. as like | D. similar"

/>

<QuizItem

  question="These unique products are not only sold domestically but also (18) ______ to global markets."
  options="A. exported | B. imported | C. destroyed | D. hidden"

  explanation="'exported to global markets' = xuất khẩu ra thị trường toàn cầu."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="19"
  question="Developing craft tourism provides employment and improves local (19) ______."
  options="A. income | B. weather | C. traffic | D. air"

<QuizItem

## PART D: WRITING & SENTENCE TRANSFORMATION (Viết & Biến Đổi Câu)

<QuizItem
  :number="21"
  question="They built this suspension bridge five years ago.
→ This suspension bridge ____________________________________________________"
  correct="This suspension bridge was built five years ago."
  explanation="Câu bị động thì Quá khứ đơn: S + was/were + V3/ed: was built five years ago."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="22"
  question="Because it rained heavily, the football match was postponed.
→ Because of ____________________________________________________"
  correct="Because of the heavy rain, the football match was postponed."
  explanation="Chuyển từ 'Because + clause' sang 'Because of + noun phrase': Because of the heavy rain..."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="23"
  question="“I will visit my grandparents in the countryside next week,” Lan said.
→ Lan said that ____________________________________________________"
  correct="Lan said that she would visit her grandparents in the countryside the following week."
  explanation="Tường thuật câu kể: I -> she, will -> would, my -> her, next week -> the following week."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="24"
  question="The test was too long for the students to finish in forty-five minutes.
→ The test was so ____________________________________________________"
  correct="The test was so long that the students couldn't finish it in forty-five minutes."
  explanation="Biến đổi 'too + adj + for sb to do' sang 'so + adj + that + S + couldn't do it'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="25"
  question="If you don't wear a helmet, you will get fined.
→ Unless ____________________________________________________"
  correct="Unless you wear a helmet, you will get fined."
  explanation="Quy đổi: If... not = Unless: Unless you wear a helmet..."
/>
