## PART B: STRUCTURES AND VOCABULARY (3.0 pts)

<QuizItem :number="5" question="My uncle suggests __________ solar panels on the roof to save electricity." options="A. install | B. to install | C. installing | D. installed" correct="C" explanation="suggest + V-ing." />

<QuizItem :number="6" question="The local festival is held annually __________ March 15th." options="A. on | B. in | C. at | D. for" correct="A" explanation="Dùng giới từ 'on' trước ngày tháng cụ thể (on March 15th)." />

<QuizItem :number="7" question="The teacher __________ taught us Grade 9 English was very caring." options="A. who | B. whom | C. which | D. whose" correct="A" explanation="who thay cho danh từ chỉ người làm chủ ngữ." />

<QuizItem :number="8" question="If we don't reduce plastic waste, ocean life __________ severely damaged." options="A. is | B. will be | C. was | D. would be" correct="B" explanation="Điều kiện loại 1: will be severely damaged." />

<QuizItem :number="9" question="She felt nervous before the exam; __________, she performed exceptionally well." options="A. however | B. therefore | C. although | D. because" correct="A" explanation="however (tuy nhiên)." />

<QuizItem :number="10" question="The internet allows students to interact __________ peers globally." options="A. with | B. to | C. at | D. on" correct="A" explanation="interact with somebody." />

<QuizItem :number="11" question="I wish our town __________ a modern community library for children." options="A. has | B. had | C. will have | D. is having" correct="B" explanation="wish + quá khứ đơn (had)." />

<QuizItem :number="12" question="The historic monument was __________ damaged during the war." options="A. severe | B. severely | C. severity | D. severeness" correct="B" explanation="severely damaged (bị tàn phá nặng nề)." />
