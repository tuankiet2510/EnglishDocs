## PART D. WRITING (3.0 pts)

### Section I. Complete each of the following sentences in such a way that it is as similar as possible in meaning to the original sentence. (2.0 pts)

<QuizItem
  :number="29"
  type="transform"
  question="It was a very exciting match. The spectators cheered wildly."
  targetPrompt="The match was so..."
  sampleAnswer="The match was so exciting that the spectators cheered wildly."
  explanation="Cấu trúc: S + be + so + adj + that + clause (Trận đấu hào hứng đến mức mà khán giả hò reo cuồng nhiệt)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="30"
  type="transform"
  question="The boy said, 'I will travel to America with my parents next spring.'"
  targetPrompt="The boy said that..."
  sampleAnswer="The boy said that he would travel to America with his parents the following spring."
  explanation="Câu tường thuật: đổi đại từ I -> he, lùi thì will -> would, next spring -> the following spring (hoặc the next spring)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="31"
  type="transform"
  question="Unless it stops raining today, we may be faced with a serious flood."
  targetPrompt="If it..."
  sampleAnswer="If it does not stop raining today, we may be faced with a serious flood."
  explanation="Công thức quy đổi: Unless + S + V = If + S + don't/doesn't + V."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="32"
  type="transform"
  question="Mr Minh is a famous Tiktoker. You met him at the clean-up club last week."
  targetPrompt="Mr Minh,..."
  sampleAnswer="Mr Minh, whom you met at the clean-up club last week, is a famous Tiktoker."
  explanation="Mệnh đề quan hệ không xác định có dấu phẩy: Mr Minh là danh từ riêng chỉ người, đóng vai trò tân ngữ -> dùng 'whom' (hoặc 'who')."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="33"
  type="transform"
  question="My father didn’t go to work yesterday because of having a bad cold."
  targetPrompt="Because my father..."
  sampleAnswer="Because my father had a bad cold, he didn’t go to work yesterday."
  explanation="Quy đổi cụm từ chỉ nguyên nhân: because of + V-ing -> because + S + V."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="34"
  type="transform"
  question="This book isn’t as interesting as that one."
  targetPrompt="That book is more..."
  sampleAnswer="That book is more interesting than this one."
  explanation="Đổi so sánh không bằng (not as ... as) sang so sánh hơn (more ... than)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="35"
  type="transform"
  question="Can you tell me who I should contact for a guided tour?"
  targetPrompt="Can you tell me who to..."
  sampleAnswer="Can you tell me who to contact for a guided tour?"
  explanation="Rút gọn mệnh đề danh từ có đại từ để hỏi: Wh-word + S + should + V -> Wh-word + to V."

### Section II. Paragraph Writing (1.0 pt)

**Đề bài:** Write a paragraph (100 - 120 words) about the following topic:  
> *“What should teenagers do to make the living environment in their area better?”*

<div class="essay-model-card">

First, they should keep their surroundings clean by putting rubbish in the right bins and not throwing litter on the streets. Second, they can join clean-up activities at school or in their neighbourhood. This helps make public places cleaner and more beautiful. In addition, teenagers should plant more trees and take care of flowers because trees give us fresh air and shade. They should also save electricity and water at home to protect natural resources. Finally, they can encourage their friends and family members to live in an environmentally friendly way. If teenagers work together, their area will become greener and cleaner.</p>
</div>
