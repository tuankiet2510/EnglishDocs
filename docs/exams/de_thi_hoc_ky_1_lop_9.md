## PART A: PHONETICS & STRESS (Ngữ Âm & Trọng Âm)

<QuizItem
  :number="1"
  question="Choose the word whose underlined part is pronounced differently from that of the others."
  options="A. community | B. bustling | C. custom | D. suburb"
  correct="A"
  explanation="'community' chữ 'u' phát âm là /juː/ (/kəˈmjuːnəti/). Các từ còn lại phát âm là /ʌ/ ('bustling' /ˈbʌslɪŋ/, 'custom' /ˈkʌstəm/, 'suburb' /ˈsʌbɜːb/)."

## PART B: VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR (Từ Vựng & Ngữ Pháp)

<QuizItem

  explanation="Cấu trúc: Wh-word + to V. 'who to ask for advice' nghĩa là hỏi ý kiến ai khi gặp căng thẳng thi cử. (Unit 3: Healthy living for teens)."

<QuizItem

<QuizItem

## PART D: WRITING & SENTENCE TRANSFORMATION (Viết & Biến Đổi Câu)

<QuizItem
  :number="33"
  question="&quot;How can I cope with anxiety before the examination?&quot; asked the student.
→ The student didn't know ____________________________________________________"
  correct="The student didn't know how to cope with anxiety before the examination."
  explanation="Chuyển sang cấu trúc rút gọn với từ để hỏi: Wh-word + to V. 'how to cope with anxiety...'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="34"
  question="If you exercise more regularly, your body becomes healthier.
→ The more ____________________________________________________"
  correct="The more regularly you exercise, the healthier your body becomes."
  explanation="So sánh kép: The more regularly you exercise, the healthier your body becomes."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="35"
  question="It's a pity that our school doesn't have an indoor swimming pool.
→ We wish ____________________________________________________"
  correct="We wish our school had an indoor swimming pool."
  explanation="Câu ước hiện tại: S + wish + S + V(quá khứ đơn: had)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="36"
  question="My father often cycled to school when he was a little boy.
→ My father used ____________________________________________________"
  correct="My father used to cycle to school when he was a little boy."
  explanation="Thói quen trong quá khứ: used to + V nguyên thể (used to cycle)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="37"
  question="They started building the community health center six months ago.
→ They have ____________________________________________________"
  correct="They have been building / have built the community health center for six months."
  explanation="Chuyển từ thì Quá khứ đơn với 'ago' sang Hiện tại hoàn thành với 'for': for six months."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="38"
  question="The workshop was so noisy that the artisans couldn't hear the instructions.
→ It was such ____________________________________________________"
  correct="It was such a noisy workshop that the artisans couldn't hear the instructions."
  explanation="Cấu trúc: 'such + a/an + adj + noun + that'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="39"
  question="Despite having a tight revision schedule, Linda managed to get 8 hours of sleep each night.
→ Although ____________________________________________________"
  correct="Although Linda had a tight revision schedule, she managed to get 8 hours of sleep each night."
  explanation="Chuyển từ 'Despite + V-ing/noun' sang 'Although + S + V'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="40"
  question="“Let’s join the neighborhood clean-up campaign this Saturday morning,” said Tom.
→ Tom suggested ____________________________________________________"
  correct="Tom suggested joining the neighborhood clean-up campaign this Saturday morning (hoặc: Tom suggested that we should join...)."
  explanation="Cấu trúc đề nghị: suggest + V-ing hoặc suggest that S + (should) + V."
/>
