## PART B: STRUCTURES AND VOCABULARY (3.0 pts)

<QuizItem :number="5" question="If I were you, I __________ join the English public speaking club." options="A. will | B. would | C. can | D. may" correct="B" explanation="Câu điều kiện loại 2: S + would + V-inf." />

<QuizItem :number="6" question="Son Doong Cave is recognized as the __________ cave in the world." options="A. large | B. larger | C. largest | D. most large" correct="C" explanation="So sánh nhất của tính từ ngắn: the largest." />

<QuizItem :number="7" question="She turned __________ the volume so that she could concentrate on her homework." options="A. up | B. down | C. on | D. off" correct="B" explanation="turn down (vặn nhỏ âm lượng)." />

<QuizItem :number="8" question="The boy __________ father is an environmental scientist won the green project." options="A. who | B. whom | C. whose | D. which" correct="C" explanation="whose father (có bố là nhà khoa học môi trường)." />

<QuizItem :number="9" question="It's essential that students __________ enough water every day." options="A. drink | B. drinks | C. drank | D. drinking" correct="A" explanation="Giả định thức: essential that S + (should) V-inf." />

<QuizItem :number="10" question="He succeeded in passing the exam __________ his thorough preparation." options="A. because | B. although | C. because of | D. in spite of" correct="C" explanation="because of + cụm danh từ." />

<QuizItem :number="11" question="We look forward to __________ you at the high school entrance ceremony." options="A. meet | B. to meet | C. meeting | D. met" correct="C" explanation="look forward to + V-ing." />

<QuizItem :number="12" question="Ba To uprising is an important historical __________ of Quang Ngai Province." options="A. event | B. time | C. day | D. year" correct="A" explanation="historical event (sự kiện lịch sử)." />
