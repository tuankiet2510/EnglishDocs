## PART B: STRUCTURES AND VOCABULARY (3.0 pts)

<QuizItem :number="5" question="She asked me if I __________ to Hue City before." options="A. went | B. have been | C. had been | D. will go" correct="C" explanation="Lùi thì trong câu gián tiếp về quá khứ hoàn thành (had been)." />

<QuizItem :number="6" question="The computer __________ was repaired yesterday functions smoothly now." options="A. who | B. which | C. whom | D. whose" correct="B" explanation="which thay cho the computer." />

<QuizItem :number="7" question="Teenagers should avoid __________ too much sugary food." options="A. eat | B. eating | C. to eat | D. ate" correct="B" explanation="avoid + V-ing." />

<QuizItem :number="8" question="The air in rural areas is much __________ than in big cities." options="A. clean | B. cleaner | C. more clean | D. cleanest" correct="B" explanation="So sánh hơn tính từ ngắn: cleaner than." />

<QuizItem :number="9" question="He was late for class __________ his alarm clock did not go off." options="A. because | B. although | C. despite | D. because of" correct="A" explanation="because + S + V." />

<QuizItem :number="10" question="Could you please show me how __________ this math problem?" options="A. solve | B. to solve | C. solving | D. solved" correct="B" explanation="how + to V-inf." />

<QuizItem :number="11" question="I wish I __________ more time to participate in community activities." options="A. have | B. had | C. will have | D. am having" correct="B" explanation="S + wish + S + V-quá khứ đơn (had)." />

<QuizItem :number="12" question="They decided to __________ their old habits and adopt a healthier lifestyle." options="A. give up | B. look after | C. put on | D. take in" correct="A" explanation="give up (từ bỏ thói quen xấu)." />

## PART D: SENTENCE TRANSFORMATION & WRITING (3.0 pts)

<QuizItem :number="26" type="sentence" originalSentence="Because of his thorough preparation, he passed the driving test easily." promptWord="Because he" correct="had prepared thoroughly, he passed the driving test easily." explanation="Chuyển từ Because of sang Because + S + V." />

<QuizItem :number="27" type="sentence" originalSentence="People expect that solar power will become cheaper in the coming decade." promptWord="Solar power" correct="is expected to become cheaper in the coming decade." explanation="Bị động khách quan: S + is expected to + V-inf." />

<QuizItem :number="28" type="sentence" originalSentence="She doesn't speak English fluently, so she cannot apply for the international scholarship." promptWord="If she" correct="spoke English fluently, she could apply for the international scholarship." explanation="Điều kiện loại 2 giả định." />

<QuizItem :number="29" type="sentence" originalSentence="The festival which was held in our village attracted thousands of tourists." promptWord="The festival held" correct="in our village attracted thousands of tourists." explanation="Rút gọn mệnh đề quan hệ bị động sang V-ed/V3 (held)." />
