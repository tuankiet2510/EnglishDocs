### Section I. Choose the word that has the underlined letter(s) pronounced differently from that of the others. (0.5 pt)

<QuizItem
  :number="1"
  question="Chọn từ có phần gạch chân phát âm khác với các từ còn lại:"

## PART B: STRUCTURES AND VOCABULARY (3.0 pts)

### Section I. Choose the letter A, B, C, or D to indicate the correct answer to each of the following questions. (2.0 pts)

<QuizItem
  :number="5"
  question="A profession in medical area is ___________ a demanding one ________ only a few can follow."
  options="A. so – that | B. such - that | C. too - that | D. enough - that"
  correct="B"
  explanation="Cấu trúc 'such + (a/an) + adj + noun + that' (a demanding one là cụm danh từ đếm được số ít)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="6"
  question="The Harbin festival, _________ first took place in 1963, is the largest ice and snow festival in the world."
  options="A. which | B. who | C. when | D. what"
  correct="A"
  explanation="Mệnh đề quan hệ không xác định có dấu phẩy: đại từ 'which' thay thế cho danh từ chỉ vật 'The Harbin festival'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="7"

  options="A. found out | B. passed down | C. came back | D. ran out of"

  explanation="Cụm động từ 'run out of' = cạn kiệt, hết sạch (hết gạo và nguyên liệu nấu ăn)."

<QuizItem
  :number="8"

<QuizItem

  options="A. was preparing | B. prepared | C. prepares | D. was prepared"

<QuizItem
  :number="11"
  question="My brother ________ me whether I was listening to him or not."

<QuizItem

### Section III. Reading Comprehension: Children's Eating Habits (1.0 pt)

<div class="reading-passage-box">
  <div class="passage-badge">📖 ĐOẠN VĂN: THÓI QUEN ĂN UỐNG CỦA TRẺ EM</div>
  <p>Children’s eating habits have changed a lot over the years. In the past, most children ate home-cooked meals prepared by their parents. Breakfast usually included rice, eggs, or porridge, while lunch and dinner often had vegetables, fish, or meat. Snacks were simple, such as fruits, peanuts, or homemade cakes. Fast food and soft drinks were rare because they were expensive and not widely available. Families often ate together, and children followed a strict meal schedule.</p>
  <p>Today, children’s eating habits are quite different. Many children prefer fast food like pizza, fried chicken, and hamburgers. They also drink more soda and sugary drinks instead of water or fresh juice. Snacks now include packaged chips, chocolates, and instant noodles. Because of busy schedules, some families no longer eat together, and children may eat in front of the TV or use their phones while eating. Although modern food choices are more convenient, health experts warn that too much fast food and sugary snacks can lead to obesity and other health problems.</p>
</div>

### Section I. Sentence Transformation (2.0 pts)

<QuizItem
  :number="29"
  type="transform"
  question="His English is not good enough to study space science."
  targetPrompt="He wishes..."
  sampleAnswer="He wishes his English were good enough to study space science."
  explanation="Câu ước ở hiện tại: S + wish + S + were/V2."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="30"
  type="transform"
  question="He wasn’t wearing a coat although it was quite cold."
  targetPrompt="Despite..."
  sampleAnswer="Despite the cold weather, he wasn’t wearing a coat."
  explanation="Đổi Although + clause -> Despite + noun phrase (the cold)."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="31"
  type="transform"
  question="Tim started taking piano lessons two years ago."
  targetPrompt="Tim has..."
  sampleAnswer="Tim has taken piano lessons for two years."
  explanation="Đổi started + V-ing ... ago -> has taken / has been taking ... for 2 years."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="32"
  type="transform"
  question="Tom was so busy that he could not talk to me."
  targetPrompt="Tom was too..."
  sampleAnswer="Tom was too busy to talk to me."
  explanation="Quy đổi so + adj + that + S + couldn't -> too + adj + (for O) + to V."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="33"
  type="transform"
  question="The woman called the police. Her house was broken into."
  targetPrompt="The woman whose..."
  sampleAnswer="The woman whose house was broken into called the police."
  explanation="Đại từ quan hệ chỉ sở hữu 'whose' thay cho 'Her house'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="34"
  type="transform"
  question="I’d rather read books than watch movies on weekends."
  targetPrompt="I prefer..."
  sampleAnswer="I prefer reading books to watching movies on weekends."
  explanation="Cấu trúc: prefer + V-ing + to + V-ing = would rather + V + than + V."
/>

<QuizItem

### Section II. Paragraph Writing (1.0 pt)

**Đề bài:** Write a paragraph (about 100–120 words) about a positive change in your lifestyle over the past three years.

<div class="essay-model-card">
  <div class="essay-badge">🏆 DÀN Ý & BÀI LUẬN MẪU THAM KHẢO</div>
  <p>Over the past three years, a remarkable positive change in my lifestyle has been my daily morning routine. In the past, I used to stay up late browsing social media, which made me feel exhausted and skip breakfast the next morning. However, two years ago, I decided to adopt a healthier habit. I started waking up early at 5:30 a.m. to jog around the local park and practice English pronunciation for thirty minutes. Additionally, I now always eat a nutritious breakfast with eggs and fresh milk. This lifestyle transformation has not only enhanced my physical health and energy levels throughout the school day, but also improved my academic performance significantly. I feel much more confident, disciplined, and optimistic about my future.</p>
</div>
