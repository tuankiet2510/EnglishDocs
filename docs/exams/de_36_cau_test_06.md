## PART B: STRUCTURES AND VOCABULARY (3.0 pts)

<QuizItem

  question="The more you practice speaking English, __________ you become."
  options="A. the most confident | B. the more confident | C. more confident | D. confident"
  correct="B"
  explanation="Cấu trúc so sánh kép (Double comparison): The + comparative..., the + comparative... (The more you practice..., the more confident...)."

## PART D: SENTENCE TRANSFORMATION & WRITING (3.0 pts)

<QuizItem
  :number="25"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="“I cannot attend the English club this afternoon because I have a math class,” Nam said."
  promptWord="Nam said that"
  correct="he could not attend the English club that afternoon because he had a math class."
  explanation="Câu gián tiếp: Lùi thì cannot -> could not, have -> had, this afternoon -> that afternoon."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="26"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="People believe that daily physical exercise reduces stress significantly."
  promptWord="Daily physical exercise"
  correct="is believed to reduce stress significantly."
  explanation="Bị động khách quan với động từ chỉ ý kiến: S + is believed to + V-inf."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="27"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="The test was too long for us to finish in 45 minutes."
  promptWord="The test was so"
  correct="long that we could not finish it in 45 minutes."
  explanation="Chuyển từ too... to sang so... that: so long that we could not finish it..."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="28"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="Unless you water these flowers regularly, they will wither."
  promptWord="If you"
  correct="do not water these flowers regularly, they will wither."
  explanation="Unless = If... not: Unless you water = If you do not water."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="29"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="I haven't visited my grandparents' hometown for five years."
  promptWord="The last time"
  correct="I visited my grandparents' hometown was five years ago."
  explanation="Biến đổi thì hiện tại hoàn thành sang quá khứ đơn với 'The last time...'."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="30"
  type="sentence"
  originalSentence="Although the traffic was congested, they arrived at the airport on time."
  promptWord="Despite"
  correct="the congested traffic, they arrived at the airport on time."
  explanation="Chuyển từ Although sang Despite + cụm danh từ: the traffic was congested -> the congested traffic."
