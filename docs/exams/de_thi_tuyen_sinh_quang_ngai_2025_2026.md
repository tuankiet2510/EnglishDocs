## PART A. PRONUNCIATION (1.0 pt)

### Section I. Choose the word (A, B, C or D) that has the underlined letter(s) pronounced differently from the others. (0.6 pt)

<QuizItem
  :number="1"
  question="Chọn từ có phần gạch chân phát âm khác với các từ còn lại:"

  explanation="yellow đọc là /oʊ/ (hoặc /əʊ/). Ba từ còn lại crowded, downtown, flower đọc là /aʊ/."

### Section I. Sentence Transformation (2.0 pts)

<QuizItem
  :number="36"
  type="transform"
  question="“Will you buy a new electric bike next week?” Anna asked me."
  targetPrompt="Anna asked me..."
  sampleAnswer="Anna asked me if/whether I would buy a new electric bike the following week."
  explanation="Câu hỏi Yes/No tường thuật: S + asked + if/whether + S + would + V + the following week."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="37"
  type="transform"
  question="The project was so challenging that no one could complete it on time."
  targetPrompt="It was such..."
  sampleAnswer="It was such a challenging project that no one could complete it on time."
  explanation="Quy đổi so ... that sang such ... that: so + adj + noun -> such + a/an + adj + noun + that."
/>

<QuizItem
  :number="38"
  type="transform"

  targetPrompt="While my mother..."
  sampleAnswer="While my mother was cooking dinner, my father came home from work."
  explanation="Quy đổi mệnh đề thời gian: When + QKĐ, QKTD -> While + QKTD, QKĐ."

<QuizItem

> *“The benefits of using electronic devices for learning English to students.”*

First and foremost, devices like laptops, tablets, and smartphones provide immediate access to vast digital resources, including online dictionaries, audio pronunciation guides, and interactive grammar exercises. Furthermore, educational mobile apps gamify the learning process, making vocabulary acquisition engaging and memorable. Electronic devices also facilitate collaboration, allowing learners to participate in international discussion forums and practice English with global peers. In conclusion, when used sensibly, electronic devices are indispensable tools that empower students to master English faster and more effectively.</p>
